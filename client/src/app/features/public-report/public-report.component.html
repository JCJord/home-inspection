Executive Summary

This section summarizes the most significant findings from the inspection, specifically focusing on Safety Hazards and Major Defects that require immediate attention or specialized evaluation.

Categories: This report divides findings into five categories: Safety Hazards (colored in red), Major Defects (in orange), Minor Defects (in yellow), Maintenance Items (in green), and Informational Items (in blue).

Safety Hazard

A condition that poses a significant risk of personal injury during normal use of the property.

Major Defect

A condition that significantly affects the habitability, safety, or primary function of a critical system.

Minor Defect

A condition that is not functioning as intended or needs repair, but does not pose a significant risk.

Maintenance Item

A routine maintenance task recommended to preserve the condition of the property or prevent future issues.

Informational

General information or observations about the property that do not require any action.
