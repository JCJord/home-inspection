2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data exclusively to:

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or your clients' data to third parties (such as real estate agents, contractors, or marketing firms). We only share data with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate the Service:

  • Payment Processing: To handle subscription billing securely.
