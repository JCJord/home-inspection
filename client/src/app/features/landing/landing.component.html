Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching to Inspectly.

Is Inspectly offline-first?

Yes, Inspectly is built offline-first. You can conduct your entire inspection, take photos, and write narratives without an internet connection. The app will sync automatically when you're back online.

How long does it take to migrate my templates?

Most inspectors can migrate their existing templates in under an hour. We provide intuitive tools to customize sections, items, and defect narratives to match your exact workflow.

Is it compatible with mobile?

Absolutely. Inspectly is designed specifically for mobile and tablet use on-site, with a clean interface that eliminates the need for 'fat-finger' corrections or endless scrolling.

Can I customize my reports?

Yes, you have full control over the layout, colors, and branding of your final PDF reports, ensuring they match your professional image perfectly.

How much does Inspectly cost?

Inspectly is free during our Early Access period. No credit card, no contracts. When we introduce pricing, early adopters will be grandfathered into the best rate.

Is my data safe? Can I export my reports?

Yes. Your inspection data is yours. Reports are exported as standard PDFs and can be downloaded at any time. We use industry-standard encryption and never share your data.
